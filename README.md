# UrbanStyle Commerce Analytics Dashboard

Sales, customer satisfaction and regional performance analysis using Power BI.

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales performance using Power BI.  
The dashboard was designed to help stakeholders understand:

- Sales performance over time
- Best-selling product categories
- Customer satisfaction trends
- Regional sales distribution
- Business KPIs for executive decision-making

The goal of this project is to simulate a real-world Business Intelligence scenario using a public e-commerce dataset.

---

## Business Objectives

The dashboard answers key business questions such as:

- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- Which states contribute the highest sales volume?
- How do sales perform throughout the year?
- What is the average customer review score?
- What is the average ticket per order?
- How efficient is delivery performance?

---

## KPIs Included

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Ticket
- Average Delivery Time
- Average Review Score

---

## Dashboard Features

### Executive KPIs
High-level business metrics displayed at the top for quick monitoring.

### Sales Trend Analysis
Monthly revenue trend visualization to identify seasonality and sales behavior.

### Category Performance
Analysis of top-performing product categories based on total sales.

### Customer Satisfaction Analysis
Average review score by category to evaluate customer experience.

### Regional Sales Analysis
Sales distribution by state to identify top-performing regions.

### Interactive Filters
Year slicer allowing users to dynamically explore the data.

## Dataset

Dataset used: Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

Main tables used:
- Orders
- Order Items
- Products
- Customers
- Reviews
- Payments
- Sellers

---

## Key Insights

- São Paulo generated the highest sales volume among all states.
- "Cool Stuff" was the top-performing category in total sales.
- Customer review scores remained consistently above 4.0.
- Sales showed a noticeable decline during September.
- The average order ticket reached $136.68.
